## Generation guardrails

- Use invented, unbranded objects only; do not reproduce a real studio, logo, product, or place.
- Keep objects plausible in scale and construction. Avoid unreadable labels, AI-generated text, and excessive staging.
- Produce no sacred, religious, royal, or ceremonial imagery as decoration.
- Keep texture, light, and materials more important than props. The visual language should feel quiet, useful, and lived-in.
- Export optimized WebP assets with stable intrinsic dimensions before replacing the CSS studies. Validate contrast and alt text in context.
